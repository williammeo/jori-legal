Privacy Policy

Jori Media Uploader · Last updated 19 November 2026

Jori Media Uploader is an internal tool operated by Dalian Jori Co., Ltd. It is used only by our own staff and only with our own TikTok account. This policy explains what the tool handles.

What the tool handles

Data Why Where it is kept
Video files we produced ourselves To deliver them to our own TikTok inbox Our internal storage; copies are sent to TikTok when we upload
TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens for our own account To call TikTok's API on our own behalf A local file on a computer we control. Never transmitted to anyone else.
Our TikTok account identifier (open_id) and display name To confirm we are uploading to the correct account The same local file

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect data about TikTok users, viewers, or any third party.
  • It does not read comments, messages, followers, or analytics.
  • It does not run analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • This website uses no cookies and no third-party scripts.

Retention

Access tokens expire automatically within hours and are refreshed only while the tool is in use. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your TikTok account's security settings, which invalidates the stored tokens immediately.

Your choices

Because the tool only operates on our own company account, there are no end-user accounts to delete. To revoke access, remove the application from your TikTok account settings, or contact us and we will delete the stored credentials.

Contact

Privacy questions: Dalian Jori Co., Ltd. Contact details are published in our TikTok developer application.
